1. Introduction

Welcome to Focus Shield: Deep Work Timer ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Proto Coders Point. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you download and use our Android application.

2. Data Collection & Personal Information

We believe your personal data belongs to you. Focus Shield is designed to operate locally on your device without registering an account or uploading your data to external servers:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or precise physical location.
  • No Account Creation: You can use all features of the app without registering or signing in.
  • Local Data Storage: Your emergency PIN, app blocking selections, session logs, and focus statistics are stored securely inside your device's local database and encrypted preferences.

3. Android Permissions & Purpose

To function effectively as a focus timer and app blocker, Focus Shield requests specific Android system permissions. These permissions are strictly used on-device for their designated features:

📊 Usage Access Permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Used strictly on-device to check which application is currently in the foreground so the app can pause distracted apps selected during an active focus session.

🖼️ Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

Used strictly on-device to display the "Protect Your Flow" blocker overlay screen when a selected distracted app is opened during a focus session.

🔔 Foreground Service & Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to keep the focus timer running in the background and display live countdown status notifications during active focus sessions.
